## Proposed addition to AGENTS.md

```markdown
## Notes agent

Write the notes for each meeting in `notes/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

**Principle.** Write the notes for a teammate who missed the meeting and has to
act on them. Record what the meeting produced: decisions, open questions, and
commitments. Don't record how the conversation went.

**Why.** The reader needs to know what is now true and what they must do. An
action nobody owns or has a date for usually doesn't happen. Who said what, and
the chatter around it, only describes how the team got to the outcome. A
decision record that names who disagreed with whom also tends to keep the
dispute alive after it was settled.

**What follows from this:**
- Every action item has one named owner and a date, because that's what makes
  it something a person can do.
- Small talk is left out. It isn't an outcome of the meeting.
- A disagreement is recorded as the decision and the reasoning behind it, not
  as who took which side.
- The notes fit on one page. If they run over, compress the discussion and keep
  the decisions and actions.

**Boundary.**
- Cut the discussion, not the substance. Keep the reasoning that changed the
  outcome, and keep any open question the group didn't resolve.
- Keep chatter that affects plans, such as "Sam is out next week."
- If no owner or date was stated in the meeting, write "owner: TBD" or
  "date: TBD" and flag it. Never make one up.
- Hard constraint: the notes are one page at most.
```

## Why this form should hold better
- Your five rules are all consequences of one idea: the notes record outcomes, not the course of the conversation. The agent can apply that idea to cases the rules don't cover. Examples are a joke in the middle of a decision, or a side debate that settles nothing.
- The reverse test turned up two failure modes in the plain rules. "Always name an owner" pushes an agent to invent one when none was stated. "No more than one page" pushes it to cut the caveat that mattered. The Boundary section covers both.
- "Don't record who disagreed" could make the agent drop the dissent entirely. The text says to keep the reasoning and unresolved questions, and to leave out only who held which position.
