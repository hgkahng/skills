I haven't changed anything. Here is a draft for the "Notes agent" section of your AGENTS.md.

**Intent (the test for whether it worked):** Someone who wasn't in the meeting can read the notes in a couple of minutes. They learn what was decided and who must do what by when. They don't learn how the conversation went.

**Draft**

```markdown
## Notes agent

Write the notes for each meeting in `notes/YYYY-MM-DD.md`.

**Principle.** The notes record outcomes: what the team decided, what it now
knows, and what it committed to do. They don't record the conversation that got
it there.

**Why.** The reader is someone acting on the meeting later, often a person who
wasn't there. Small talk and who-said-what are part of how the meeting went, and
they don't help that reader act. Attributing disagreements also makes people
guarded in the room and turns the notes into evidence. A commitment only exists
if someone owns it and there's a time to check it. An action without both is a
wish.

**Hence:**
- Small talk never appears, because it isn't an outcome.
- Disagreement appears as the decision that resolved it, never as who opposed whom.
- Every action item reads "[Owner] will [do X] by [date]".
- The notes fit on one page, because outcomes are short. If they don't fit,
  you're recording conversation. Cut that, not the actions.

**Boundary.**
- Cut padding, not substance. If a concern raised in the meeting is still open
  or changed the decision, keep it, unattributed ("Open risk: …").
- Never invent an owner or date. If the meeting didn't settle one, write
  "Owner: unassigned" or "Date: proposed [date]" so the gap is visible, and
  don't guess.
- The page limit never justifies dropping an action item.
```

**How the five rules fall out:**
- No small talk, no recorded disagreements, and the page limit all follow from "outcomes, not conversation".
- Owner and date follow from "a commitment needs an owner and a time".

**What the draft adds:** Taken literally, "always name an owner" and "put a date on every item" push an agent to fabricate when the meeting didn't settle either. "Don't record disagreement" could also make it drop a real risk. The Boundary lines cover both.

**Placement:** This is identity-level guidance for one agent in a folder that only does this job, so AGENTS.md is the right place. A skill would only make sense if the folder had other agents. I can apply it to AGENTS.md if you'd like.
