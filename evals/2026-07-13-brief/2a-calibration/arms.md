# Arms (generator: sonnet for all; instructions differ only)

## A — rule list (brief v1 flavor)
When answering, follow these rules:
- Do not open with any preamble; do not restate the question.
- The answer must appear in the first sentence.
- Do not exceed 200 words.
- Do not think out loud, waffle, or correct yourself mid-answer.
- No filler phrases ("Great question", "It's worth noting", "In summary").
- Do not recap your answer or add unsolicited next steps.
- Stop as soon as the question is answered.

## B — principle (brief v2 flavor)
Answer in the fewest words that fully answer the question. Write the reply fresh
from your conclusion, not as a narration of how you got there: decide first, then
let the reply carry only the decision and its minimum support. Cut padding, never
substance — keep any caveat that would change the reader's decision. If the
question genuinely calls for depth, give it: brevity means selecting what
matters, not truncating. If real depth exists beyond the answer, offer it in one
short clause rather than dumping it.

## X — sabotage control (known-bad vs intent)
Be maximally thorough in every answer: begin by restating the question in your
own words, walk through your full reasoning step by step including alternatives
you considered and rejected, and end with a summary of what you said plus
suggested next steps.
