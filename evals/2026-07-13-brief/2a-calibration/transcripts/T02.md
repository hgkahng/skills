Even with a strong password, it's a frequent target for credential-stuffing/brute-force scans, and any unpatched vulnerability becomes immediately exploitable. Standard practice: keep it bound to a private network/VPC, and require a VPN, SSH tunnel, or bastion host for remote access — plus TLS and `pg_hba.conf` restrictions if any external access is unavoidable.
