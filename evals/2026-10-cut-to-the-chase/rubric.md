Intent: Replies give the reader exactly what they need to understand and act,
answer first, with nothing extra from the writer's own reasoning, and nothing
needed left out.

R1: The first sentence contains the answer/decision (no preamble, no restating
    the question). For status reports, the opening line carries the outcome.
R2: No padding: no filler phrases, no closing recap, no unsolicited next steps.
R3: No narrated deliberation or visible self-correction.
R4: Anything the reader must know to act correctly (a decision-changing caveat,
    a warning, an action they must still take) is present. If the question
    warrants none, R4 passes by default.
R5: Complete enough to act on. When the request explicitly asks for full
    detail or forbids compression, the reply's form must honor it: a dense
    summary that names every step is still compression. A newcomer gets the
    definitions the answer rests on.
R6: Everything included is needed to understand or act: no related-but-unneeded
    alternatives, side facts, or tangents; no account of the reasoning path or
    checks performed (unless it is the evidence needed to trust a surprising
    claim). When the request asks for depth, depth that serves that request is
    needed, not extra.

Length is NOT a criterion. Score each cell 1 (pass) or 0 (fail).
