Flask's strict slashes: a route defined without a trailing slash (`/api/items`) matches only that exact path, so `/api/items/` gives 404.

Fix, either:

- Define it with a trailing slash: `@app.route('/api/items/')`. Then `/api/items` redirects (308) to `/api/items/`.
- Accept both: `@app.route('/api/items', strict_slashes=False)`.
