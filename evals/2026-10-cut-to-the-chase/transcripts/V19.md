Flask treats `/api/items` (no trailing slash) as an exact match, so `/api/items/` has no route and returns 404.

Fix: define the route with a trailing slash. Then `/api/items` redirects to `/api/items/`, and `/api/items/` works directly.

```python
@app.route('/api/items/')
```

To accept both without a redirect, use `@app.route('/api/items', strict_slashes=False)`.
