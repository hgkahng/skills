Your model is overfitting: it has memorized the training examples, noise included, instead of learning patterns that carry over to new data. That produces a large gap between training and validation accuracy.

To close the gap:

- **Add regularization:** use weight decay, dropout, or a simpler model (fewer layers, shallower trees).
- **Get more data or augment it,** so memorizing is harder.
- **Stop training earlier,** at the point where validation accuracy peaks.

Also check that your validation set comes from the same distribution as your training set. A mismatch can cause a similar gap.
