Intent: A smart reader who does not know the field can follow the reply on the
first read, and it is still correct and complete.

Q1 Jargon: every technical term is either avoided (a plain word is used) or
   explained in plain words at first use. A term the user themselves asked about
   or used may be kept, but must be explained unless the user clearly knows it.
Q2 No idioms or figures of speech (for example "boils down to", "under the
   hood", "a ton of", "sweet spot", "bake in"). Plain literal language only.
Q3 Accurate: nothing in the reply is wrong, and simplifying did not change the
   meaning of a claim.
Q4 Literals intact: any code, command, file name or error text from the request
   appears exactly as given. Passes by default if the request has none.
Q5 Complete: the reply has what the reader needs to understand the answer and
   act on it. Missing an essential idea fails.
Q6 Natural: it reads like an ordinary person talking to a peer. It is not
   stilted, childish, or condescending, and it does not talk down to an expert
   who asked in expert terms.

Length is NOT a criterion. Score each cell 1 (pass) or 0 (fail).
