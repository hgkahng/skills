Here is a Beamer-ready RAG pipeline figure (16:9 slide, slide styling: thicker lines, `\small` labels, high-contrast colors). The five sources sit in one vertical column, the retriever is vertically centred on that column, and the flow continues left to right into the LLM and the answer.

```latex
% Forward-pass order (left to right):
%   PDFs / Web pages / SQL database / Slack / Email
%     -> Retriever -> LLM -> Answer
\documentclass[aspectratio=169,11pt]{beamer}
\usepackage{tikz}
\usetikzlibrary{arrows.meta, positioning, calc}
\setbeamertemplate{navigation symbols}{}

% --- Unified palette: one named style per box type, one arrow style ---
\tikzset{
  srcbox/.style={draw=gray!70, fill=gray!10, line width=1.2pt, rounded corners=3pt,
                 minimum width=2.9cm, minimum height=0.85cm,
                 font=\small, align=center},
  procbox/.style={draw=blue!65, fill=blue!10, line width=1.2pt, rounded corners=3pt,
                  minimum width=2.0cm, minimum height=5.45cm,
                  font=\small\bfseries, align=center},
  llmbox/.style={draw=blue!65, fill=blue!10, line width=1.2pt, rounded corners=4pt,
                 minimum width=1.8cm, minimum height=1.4cm,
                 font=\small\bfseries, align=center},
  ansbox/.style={draw=green!45!black, fill=green!12, line width=1.2pt, rounded corners=4pt,
                 minimum width=2.2cm, minimum height=1.4cm,
                 font=\small\bfseries, align=center},
  annot/.style={font=\scriptsize\itshape, text=gray!80!black, align=center},
  arr/.style={-{Latex[length=2.6mm]}, line width=1.2pt, color=gray!75!black},
}

\begin{document}
\begin{frame}[plain]
\vfill
\centering
\begin{tikzpicture}[node distance=0.3cm]

  % --- Five sources, vertical column ---
  \node[srcbox]                  (pdf)   {PDFs};
  \node[srcbox, below=of pdf]    (web)   {Web pages};
  \node[srcbox, below=of web]    (sql)   {SQL database};
  \node[srcbox, below=of sql]    (slack) {Slack};
  \node[srcbox, below=of slack]  (mail)  {Email};
  \node[annot, below=2mm of mail] {raw sources};

  % --- Retriever, vertically centred on the column ---
  \node[procbox, anchor=west] (ret)
      at ($(pdf.east)!0.5!(mail.east)+(1.5cm,0)$) {Retriever};

  % --- LLM and answer, on the retriever's centre line ---
  \node[llmbox, right=1.6cm of ret] (llm) {LLM};
  \node[ansbox, right=1.3cm of llm] (ans) {Answer};
  \node[annot, below=2mm of ans]    {grounded in\\retrieved text};

  % --- Arrows: each source enters the retriever at its own height ---
  \foreach \s in {pdf, web, sql, slack, mail}
    \draw[arr] (\s.east) -- (ret.west |- \s.east);

  % --- Arrows: retriever -> LLM -> answer ---
  \draw[arr] (ret.east) -- node[annot, above=1pt] {top-$k$\\chunks} (llm.west);
  \draw[arr] (llm.east) -- (ans.west);

\end{tikzpicture}
\vfill
\end{frame}
\end{document}
```

Assumptions and notes:
- Slide styling was chosen because you asked for a Beamer figure: 1.2pt lines, `\small` labels, nothing smaller than `\scriptsize`.
- The sources use a single vertical column (no 2x2 grid), so every arrow runs horizontally into the retriever without crossing a node.
- Color follows the usual semantics: gray for inputs, blue for processing, green for the final answer. The figure also reads fine in grayscale.
- To use it inside an existing deck, copy the `\tikzset` block and the `tikzpicture` into your own frame. You need the `arrows.meta`, `positioning` and `calc` libraries.
