Here is a standalone TikZ figure of the encoder block, styled for a NeurIPS paper (thin 0.5pt lines, `\footnotesize` labels, grayscale-safe fills, and dashed residual paths so it still reads in B&W).

Assumptions:
- The forward pass runs bottom to top: Input Embedding, Multi-Head Attention, Add & Norm, Feed-Forward, Add & Norm.
- Each residual leaves the input of its sublayer (the dot on the incoming arrow). It runs up the right side and enters the Add & Norm that closes that sublayer.
- I left out positional encoding because you didn't list it. It is one extra node if you want it.

```latex
\documentclass[tikz,border=4pt]{standalone}
\usetikzlibrary{arrows.meta, positioning, calc}

% Forward-pass order (bottom -> top):
%   Input Embedding -> Multi-Head Attention -> Add & Norm
%                   -> Feed-Forward         -> Add & Norm
% Residual 1: input of Multi-Head Attention -> second input of first Add & Norm
% Residual 2: input of Feed-Forward         -> second input of second Add & Norm

\tikzset{
  % one base box style, three named variants
  blk/.style={draw=gray!70, rounded corners=2pt, line width=0.5pt,
              minimum width=3.4cm, minimum height=0.8cm,
              font=\footnotesize, align=center},
  embbox/.style={blk, fill=gray!14},
  subbox/.style={blk, draw=blue!55, fill=blue!9},
  normbox/.style={blk, fill=yellow!16},
  annot/.style={font=\scriptsize\itshape, text=black!65, align=center},
  % a single arrow style; residual paths differ only by dashing
  arr/.style={-{Latex[length=1.8mm,width=1.4mm]}, line width=0.5pt, color=black!70},
  res/.style={arr, dash pattern=on 3pt off 2pt},
  dot/.style={circle, fill=black!70, inner sep=0pt, minimum size=2.2pt},
}

\begin{document}
\begin{tikzpicture}[node distance=0.75cm]

% --- Main flow, bottom to top ---
\node[embbox]  (emb) {Input Embedding};
\node[subbox,  above=of emb] (mha) {Multi-Head Attention};
\node[normbox, above=of mha] (an1) {Add \& Norm};
\node[subbox,  above=of an1] (ffn) {Feed-Forward};
\node[normbox, above=of ffn] (an2) {Add \& Norm};

% --- Entry / exit arrows with anchored labels ---
\coordinate (in)  at ($(emb.south)-(0,0.75)$);
\coordinate (out) at ($(an2.north)+(0,0.75)$);
\draw[arr] (in) -- (emb.south);
\node[annot, below=1mm of in] {input tokens};
\draw[arr] (an2.north) -- (out);
\node[annot, above=1mm of out] {encoder output};

% --- Junctions at the input of each sublayer (where the residual branches off) ---
\coordinate (j1) at ($(emb.north)!0.5!(mha.south)$);
\coordinate (j2) at ($(an1.north)!0.5!(ffn.south)$);

% --- Main-flow arrows ---
\draw[arr] (emb.north) -- (mha.south);
\draw[arr] (mha.north) -- (an1.south);
\draw[arr] (an1.north) -- (ffn.south);
\draw[arr] (ffn.north) -- (an2.south);

% --- Residuals on the right side, routed with |- ---
\coordinate (rx) at ($(mha.east)+(0.8,0)$);   % common x for both residual rails
\node[dot] at (j1) {};
\node[dot] at (j2) {};
\draw[res] (j1) -- (j1 -| rx) |- (an1.east);
\draw[res] (j2) -- (j2 -| rx) |- (an2.east);

% --- Residual labels, offset to the right with pointer arrows ---
\path (j1 -| rx) -- (an1.east -| rx) coordinate[midway] (m1);
\path (j2 -| rx) -- (an2.east -| rx) coordinate[midway] (m2);
\node[annot, right=1.1cm of m1] (lab1) {residual};
\node[annot, right=1.1cm of m2] (lab2) {residual};
\draw[arr] (lab1.west) -- (m1);
\draw[arr] (lab2.west) -- (m2);

\end{tikzpicture}
\end{document}
```

Notes:
- All colors come from the `\tikzset` palette at the top. Box types differ in fill lightness as well as hue, so they stay distinguishable in grayscale.
- Both residual rails share one x-position (`rx`), so they never cross. The two vertical segments occupy different y-ranges.
- To place it in your paper, copy the `\tikzset` into your preamble. Paste the `tikzpicture` into a `figure` environment with `\centering`, `\caption{...}` and `\label{fig:encoder}`. Add `\usetikzlibrary{arrows.meta, positioning, calc}` to the preamble. Inside the paper the labels will use your document font, so they match the body text.
