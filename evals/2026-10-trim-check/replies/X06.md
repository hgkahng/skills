The data are synthetic: training loss decays smoothly, validation loss bottoms out at epoch 22 (0.400) and then climbs, which is where the annotation points.

Notes:
- **Context assumed:** paper (single-column width, 0.4 to 0.8 pt lines, `\footnotesize` text). If this is for a Beamer slide, I would thicken the lines and enlarge the fonts.
- **Data:** purely made up. Replace the two `coordinates` lists with your logged values. If the validation minimum moves, update the marker coordinate and the arrow target (`22,0.400`) and the label text.
- **Black-and-white printing:** the curves differ in dash pattern as well as color. The legend sits in the upper-right corner, where both curves are far below it.
- **Styles:** everything is set in `\tikzset` / `\pgfplotsset` at the top, so changing a color or line weight is a one-line edit. For a `figure` environment, drop the `\documentclass`/`\begin{document}` wrapper and put the `tikzpicture` inside `\begin{figure} ... \caption{...}\label{fig:loss} \end{figure}`.
